How to Play

4 simple moves to master untangling.

1. Tap to Release Tap any arrow that has a clear path forward to launch it off the board.
2. Avoid Collisions Hitting another arrow costs a life (♥). Release the blocking arrows first!
3. Hold to Preview Press & hold any arrow to test its trajectory (Green = clear, Red = blocked). Release to cancel!
